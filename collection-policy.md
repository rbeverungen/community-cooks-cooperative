Collection Policy
Mission
The Community Cooks Cooperative preserves and shares materials that help people gather around food, music, stories, and culture.

Primary Collection
 Cookbooks
 Food history
 Regional foodways
 Gardening
 Food preservation
 Baking
 Fermentation
 
Secondary Collection
 Vinyl records
 CDs
 DVDs
 Food and music documentaries

 
Selection Principles
Preference is given to materials that:

 Encourage creativity
 Support learning
 Reflect local and regional culture
 Foster community connection
 Are difficult to discover through streaming platforms alone
