Community Cooks Cooperative
A community-owned lending collection focused on cookbooks, food culture, music, and shared knowledge.

Mission
To preserve, share, and discover the books, recipes, recordings, and cultural works that bring people together around tables and around speakers. To maintain and improve foodways, relationships with the environment,and public health.

Principles
Community-owned
Privacy-respecting
Open-source
No advertising
No data brokers
Long-term preservation
Initial Collection
Cookbooks
Vinyl records
CDs
DVDs
Phase 0 Goals
Catalog 25 cookbooks
Catalog 10 records
Recruit 3–5 participants
Test lending workflows
Develop community metadata
Status
